A Cleaner and Better School 🧹

Module 4 · Week 46 — Community, Travel, and Future Ideas

2. Vocabulary 📖

Words for talking about school problems and solutions. Tap a card for an example.

litter🗑️
Rubbish or trash left on the ground.
"There was litter all over the playground after lunch."
recycle♻️
To use waste material again instead of throwing it away.
"We should recycle paper and plastic bottles at school."
facility🏫
A building, room or piece of equipment used for a purpose.
"Our school needs better sports facilities."
pollution🏭
Dirty or harmful air, water or environment.
"Traffic pollution near the school gate is a big problem."
suggest💡
To give an idea for other people to think about.
"I suggest putting more bins around the yard."
improve📈
To make something better than it was before.
"We can improve the canteen by adding healthier food."
responsible🙋
Having a duty to take care of something.
"Every student should be responsible for their own rubbish."
campaign📢
A series of organised actions to achieve a goal.
"Our class started a campaign to reduce plastic use."
volunteer🤝
To offer to help with something without being paid.
"Many students volunteer to clean the schoolyard on Fridays."
solution🔧
An answer to a problem.
"Planting more trees is a simple solution to the heat problem."

4. Role-Play 🎭

Act these out with a partner. Tap Show hints if you get stuck.

🎬 Scenario 1 — Reporting a problem to the Student Council

You are worried about the litter near the school gate. Talk to a student council member, explain the problem, and suggest two solutions.

🧑 Student A — Concerned student🧑 Student B — Student council member
  • A: "Hi, can I talk to you about the litter near the gate?"
  • B: "Sure, what's the problem exactly?"
  • A: "There's rubbish everywhere after school. I think we need more bins."
  • B: "That's a good idea. What else could we do?"
  • A: "Maybe we could start a small campaign to remind students."
  • B: "I'll suggest it to the teachers. Thanks for telling me!"

🎬 Scenario 2 — Planning a class clean-up day

Your classroom and yard area are getting messy. Plan a clean-up day together — decide what to do, who does what, and when.

🧑 Student A — Class monitor🧑 Student B — Classmate
  • A: "Our classroom is really messy. Should we organise a clean-up day?"
  • B: "Good idea! When should we do it?"
  • A: "How about this Saturday morning?"
  • B: "Okay, I can bring gloves and bags."
  • A: "Great, I'll ask other classmates to help too."
  • B: "Let's also plant a few flowers to make it nicer."

🎭 4. Role-Play

Time to act! Choose a role with your partner and solve the problem together.

Scenario A: Building a Lego Castle 🏰

Situation: You and your partner are building a giant Lego castle, but you only have a few red blocks left. You both want to use them for different things.

  • Role A: The "Idea Person" (wants to make a tall tower).
  • Role B: The "Builder" (wants to make a strong gate).
  • 👤 Role A: "I think we should use the red blocks for the tower because..."
  • 👤 Role B: "That's a good idea, but what if we..."
  • 🤝 Together: "Let's compromise! We can..."

Scenario B: The Group Project 📚

Situation: You are doing a science project, but one person hasn't done their drawing yet, and the project is due tomorrow!

  • Role A: The Leader (needs the drawing to finish the poster).
  • Role B: The Student (forgot how to draw the picture and is shy to ask).
  • 👤 Role A: "Hi! Do you have your drawing? We really need it."
  • 👤 Role B: "I'm sorry, I'm stuck. I don't know how to start."
  • 🤝 Role A: "That's okay! How can I help you?"

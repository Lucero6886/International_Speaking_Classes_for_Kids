Working in a Team! 🤝

Module 3 · Week 31 — School, Skills, and Healthy Choices

🎯 Today I can…

  • 🤝 Talk about working in a team.
  • 📋 Say my job or task in a group.
  • ✅ Help my group choose one idea together.

1. Let's Start! 🧊

Say your answer out loud. Then tap the card to see an example. 😊

👩‍🏫 Teacher: Smile and wave. Let kids answer by voice OR with a Zoom reaction. Model each answer first.

3. Let's Talk! 🗣️

Answer each question. Need help? Tap 💡 Ideas or the Show me button.

👩‍🏫 Teacher: Ask, wait, and give a thumbs up for every try. Put kids in small groups to plan one class task together.

4. Let's Act! 🎭

Play with a friend. Tap Show words if you need help.

🎬 Choose a class job

Work with a friend. Choose jobs for a class poster together.

👑 Leader🧒 Team member

Leader: "Let's choose jobs for our poster."

Member: "I want to draw the picture."

Leader: "OK! You draw. I will write."

Member: "Great! Let's start now."

🎬 Solve a small problem

Your team has two ideas. Talk and choose one idea together.

🧒 You🧒 Friend

You: "We have two ideas. What do we do?"

Friend: "Let's vote! Put up your hand for idea one."

You: "OK, idea two wins. Let's do idea two."

Friend: "Good idea! Let's start."

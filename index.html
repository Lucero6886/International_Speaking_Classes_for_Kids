Show and Tell: My Special Object 🎁

Module 3 · Week 32 — School, Skills, and Healthy Choices

🎯 Today I can…

  • 🎁 Describe my special object (colour, size, old or new).
  • 📖 Tell a short story about my object.
  • 🗣️ Ask and answer questions about objects.

1. Let's Start! 🧊

Say your answer out loud. Then tap the card to see an example. 😊

👩‍🏫 Teacher: Ask kids to grab one small object near them before class starts (a toy, a cup, a pencil). Model each answer first.
👋
Hello! Do you have an object near you?
Say:"Yes! I have my cup."
🎒
What is in your bag today?
Say:"I have a book and a pencil case."
🖐️
Can you hold up one object?
Say:"Yes! This is my toy car."
😊
Are you ready for Show and Tell?
Say:"Yes, I am ready!"
🌟
Do you like Show and Tell?
Say:"Yes, I like Show and Tell!"

3. Let's Talk! 🗣️

Answer each question. Need help? Tap 💡 Ideas or the Show me button.

👩‍🏫 Teacher: Ask, wait, and clap for every try. Put kids in pairs to ask "What is your special object?" to each other.

4. Let's Act! 🎭

Play with a friend. Tap Show words if you need help.

🎬 Show and tell time

Show your special object to a friend. Tell them about it.

🧒 You🧒 Friend

You: "Look! This is my ___."

Friend: "Wow! What colour is it?"

You: "It is ___."

Friend: "Is it old or new?"

You: "It is ___. I got it from ___."

🎬 Guess my object

Give clues about your object. Your friend guesses what it is.

🧒 You🧒 Friend

You: "I have a special object. It is ___ (colour)."

Friend: "Is it a toy?"

You: "Yes, it is! / No, it is not."

Friend: "Can I see it?"

You: "Yes! Look, this is my ___."

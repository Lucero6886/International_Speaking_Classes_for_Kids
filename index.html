4. Let's Act! 🎭

Play with a friend. Tap Show words if you need help.

🎬 Sharing is caring

Ask to share crayons with a classmate. Say thank you.

🧒 You🧒 Classmate

You: "Can I share your crayons, please?"

Classmate: "Yes, sure! Here you are."

You: "Thank you! You are very kind."

Classmate: "You're welcome!"

🎬 Oops! Sorry!

You broke your classmate's pencil by accident. Say sorry and fix it.

🧒 You🧒 Classmate

You: "Oh no! Sorry, I broke your pencil."

Classmate: "It's okay. Accidents happen."

You: "I will give you a new one."

Classmate: "Thank you! You are a good friend."
